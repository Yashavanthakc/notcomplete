SOMETHING FROM MY HEART

Open My Heart

♡

💌 Tap the envelope to open my heart

✦ ❤️ ✦

My dear Varsha,

You always ask me, "Why me? There are so many beautiful girls in this world. Why only me?"

The reason I like you so much is because of the time, value, care, love, and feelings you gave me.

I got to experience all of that, even if it was only for a short time. I don't know how I fell in love with you so deeply in such a short period of time.

I don't know whether you will be with me for the rest of my life or not. But I feel like I won't be able to love anyone else in this lifetime the way I love you.

Still, I'm not going to force you to stay with me. You have to decide for yourself who you want in your life.

The sooner you make a decision, the better it will be for both of our lives. As we grow older, time keeps passing, so don't miss out on enjoying and living this beautiful age of your life.

In the end, the decision is completely yours.

I only wanted you to know what is inside my heart.

Always, Yashavantha ❤️
